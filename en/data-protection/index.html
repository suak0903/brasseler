Data protection:

Your privacy

Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co. KG respects your privacy and your personal rights. We want to provide our customers with the best possible service, and protect their personal data and their interests effectively. The term “data” includes all information that would facilitate or make possible the unique identification of any person. We always comply with the regulations of data protection law and other relevant specifications.

Just like Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co. KG itself, this privacy policy is subject to ongoing development and modification. Please review this document regularly to check for any changes or additions.

Your data

You can access the Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co. KG website without providing your personal information. We will only request and use information from you when absolutely necessary to handle your inquiry and provide you with complete information and service. When you visit our website, our server saves the IP address of your internet service provider, the website from which you access our page, the pages of our site you visit, and the date and duration of your visit as standard information.

Any further personal data is saved only if you provide it to us voluntarily, for instance when completing registration, submitting an order, sending an inquiry via e-mail, etc.

Personal data includes all information that refers to an identified or identifiable natural person. A person is considered identifiable if they can be identified either directly or indirectly, in particular through association with a designation such as a name, etc. Your data will be securely transmitted in encrypted form using state of the art technology, and stored only on the servers of Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co. KG.

Personal data such as your last name, first name, and e-mail address is used for this purpose. Collected data is used only for the purpose of processing your inquiry or concern.

Mandatory details are specifically identified on the forms with an * (asterisk). All further information is voluntary.

Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co.KG will not disclose the personal data collected using the form to third parties without your explicit permission, nor will it disclose your personal data to other departments/areas not involved in the purpose for which the form is used. Your data will only be used by the department responsible for the purpose of the form. Personal data collected with the form will be deleted when you request Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co.KG to do so, and/or when required by law.

Data transmission to third parties

Data is not transmitted to third parties without your consent. Personal data is only collected and transmitted to state institutions and agencies which require the information in accordance with the law, or if we are required to do so by a court order. We have obligated all of our employees and service provider companies to maintain confidentiality and comply with data protection provisions.

Logging when you access our website

Each time our website is accessed, or each time a file stored on our website is accessed, this access is logged. This information is saved for internal, system-related and statistical purposes. The following information is logged: Name of the accessed file, date and time of access, transmitted quantity of data, notification that the file was accessed, web browser and requesting domain. In addition, the IP addresses of the requesting computer are included in the log.

Social networks

Social network plug-ins are designated on our website with a symbol for the specific web service. If you click a link to an external social network on our website, responsibility will be transferred to the operator of that platform. The operator is also responsible for the content of the pages accessed: Because the page is linked to the server of the social network, information that you have accessed our website is stored, even if you are not logged in. This information (including IP address) is transmitted directly to the operator of the platform and stored at the operator’s location. Please see the data protection information of the operator for further information on privacy, on the scope of data collected, and on how data is processed and used by the social network.

If you are already logged into the social network and click the plug-in on our website, this action will be linked directly to your profile. In addition, the information you provide will be published directly on your profile. To prevent this information from being linked to your profile, please log out before visiting our website.

Cookies

We use cookies to optimise our website and our online services, and to better address your needs. Cookies cannot read or destroy information on your hard drive. They are only data elements saved on your browser to make your visit to our website as simple and interesting as possible. It is not possible to identify individual customers using cookies. If you do not consent to the use of cookies, please change your browser settings so that no cookies will be saved.

Google Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service of Google Inc. (“Google”). Google Analytics uses “cookies”, text files that are saved on your computer and that facilitate an analysis of your use of the website. The information generated by the cookies regarding your use of the website is generally transmitted to a Google server in the USA and stored there.

If IP anonymisation is activated on this website, however, Google will first abbreviate your IP address within the member states of the European Union or another contracting state to the Agreement on the European Economic Area. The complete IP address will only be transmitted to a Google server in the USA and abbreviated there in exceptional cases. Google will use this information on behalf of the operator of this website to analyse your use of the website, in order to compile reports on website activities and provide other services to the website operator related to use of the website and internet usage. The IP address transmitted by your browser to Google Analytics will not be combined with other data by Google. You can prevent cookies from being saved by changing the settings in your browser software; however, please note that if you do so, you may not be able to use all functions of this website in full. Furthermore, you can prevent the data generated by the cookie related to your use of the website (including your IP address) from being recorded by Google and the processing of this data by Google by downloading and installing the browser plug-in available at the following link: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout?hl=de

Use and transmission of your data

Your data is only used to respond to the current inquiry, and
is not stored beyond the term of the inquiry without your consent. Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co.KG will not provide your data to third parties except for our affiliated companies, nor trade or sell your data, etc. without your prior consent. Personal data will only be recorded or transmitted to state institutions and official agencies as required by law.
We obligate our employees/business partners to maintain confidentiality.

External links

In some cases, Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co.KG refers to third-party websites through external links. We work to choose these links carefully, but we cannot accept any liability for the contents of these websites, nor for ensuring they comply with data protection law regulations. In light of this, we hereby distance ourselves from all content on such pages.

Protection of children and young people

There is no information on our website directly intended for children under 13 years of age; likewise, we do not intentionally collect information from children younger than 13. Please ensure that your children use the internet responsibly, and do not provide any data to us without your consent.

Right to information

Upon request, Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co.KG will inform you of what personal data we have saved on you. If your saved information is not up to date, we will correct this information on your request. Likewise, we will delete your data if you request that we do so.

Data security

Gebr. Brasseler GmbH & Co. KG accepts responsibility for the data we collect. A variety of technical and organisational measures help protect the security and confidentiality of your data.

Your rights:

You have the right

  • to information in accordance with Article 15 GDPR,
  • to rectification in accordance with Article 16 GDPR,
  • to deletion in accordance with Article 17 GDPR,
  • to restrict processing in accordance with Article 18 GDPR,
  • to data portability in accordance with Article 20 GDPR.

You also have the right to submit complaints regarding the way we process your data to a data protection supervisory authority.

Please note that you may revoke any consent you provide to us under data privacy law at any time, with future effect. If you do revoke your consent, our services may no longer be available to you, or may only be available to a limited extent.
