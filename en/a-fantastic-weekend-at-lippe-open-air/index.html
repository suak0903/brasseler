A Fantastic Weekend at Lippe Open Air

Published on 05 August 2024

Lemgo, 05 August, 2024

What an amazing festival weekend! We are thrilled by the lively atmosphere and the energy that Lippe Open Air has once again brought to our region. One thing is certain: Lippe knows how to celebrate!

We have been sponsors of Lippe Open Air for many years. “As an internationally operating company, our region is especially close to our hearts. Our commitment as sponsors reflects our deep connection to Lemgo and the East Westphalia-Lippe region. We want to contribute to the quality of life in our home region and support local events that strengthen our community,” emphasised Klaus Rübesamen, Managing Director.

A heartfelt thank you goes out to the dedicated team of Lippe Open Air for the excellent organisation! It is particularly noteworthy that the entire organisation team works on a voluntary basis and that all proceeds are consistently donated to a sustainable cause. Celebrating and doing good, that’s Lippe Open Air.
