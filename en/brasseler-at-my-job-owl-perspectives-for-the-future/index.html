Brasseler at my job OWL – Perspectives for the Future

Published on 20 March 2025

Lemgo, 20 March, 2025

Why is Brasseler worth considering as an employer? Because we offer perspectives for the future – for apprentices, career starters, and professionals. At the “my job OWL” fair, we had insightful conversations with interested candidates about exactly that.

“At Brasseler, we don’t think in terms of short-term jobs, but in long-term careers. Many of our employees stay with us for decades. As a mid-sized company with a global presence, we combine international opportunities with a family-oriented work environment. Together, we set standards in medical technology, and those who work with us contribute to improving patient care worldwide,” emphasizes Björn Hartmann, Head of Human Resources.

If you’re interested in a future with Brasseler, mark your calendar for May:that’s when the application phase for our 2026 apprenticeships begins.
