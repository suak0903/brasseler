Brasseler Becomes one of the New Main Sponsors of Lemgo Marketing

Published on 11 February 2025

Lemgo, 11 February 2025

As a family-owned company with deep roots in Lemgo, we take responsibility for our region. In 2025, we will support numerous city festivals and events as a main sponsor of Lemgo Marketing, enriching life in our city.

Lemgo Marketing organizes a wide range of events each year that contribute to the region’s appeal. These include the Easter Market, the Strohsemmel Festival, the Abteigarten Festival, the Bruch Market, Moonlight Shopping events, the Lemgo Children’s Theater, the children’s flea market at Lippegarten, and the Lemgo Ice and Winter World.

“Our commitment as a sponsor reflects our strong connection to Lemgo. We actively want to contribute to making the city vibrant, attractive, and future-proof. By supporting regional initiatives, we strengthen the community and promote Lemgo as a place to live and do business. As a family company, we take responsibility for the future of our region and the people who live and work here,” explains Stephan Köhler, Chairman of the Management Board.

Events like these bring people together, revitalize the city center, and create memorable experiences for all generations.

Also looking forward to this partnership, Laura Fuchs, Manager of Corporate Communications, shares her enthusiasm: “Year after year, Lemgo Marketing creates incredible experiences for our main location. With creative ideas for diverse events, their team delivers something special for the people in our region. This dedication aligns with our values as a family company, and we are delighted to support it.”

We are ready for an exciting and eventful 2025 in Lemgo!
