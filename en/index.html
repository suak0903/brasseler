Brasseler – Medizintechnik made in Lemgo

As a family-owned company, Brasseler has stood for more than 100 years for improving people’s quality of life around the world through its products for dentistry and surgery.

What began in 1923 with a visionary idea has grown into an international success story: Brasseler has developed into a globally operating MedTech company based in East Westphalia-Lippe and is now one of the most important employers in the region.

Brasseler has been family-owned since its founding. The company’s shared foundation of values is shaped by long-term thinking and a strong focus on people. Today, Brasseler employs around 1,500 people worldwide, including 1,200 at its headquarters in Lemgo.
