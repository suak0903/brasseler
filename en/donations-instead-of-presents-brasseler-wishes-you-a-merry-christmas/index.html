Donations Instead of Presents – Brasseler Wishes You a Merry Christmas

Published on 23 December 2024

The holiday season is a special time to pause and give back. As in previous years, we are supporting a meaningful project in our region with a donation.

This Christmas, we are donating to Fruchtalarm, an initiative that brings joy to critically ill children through mobile cocktail bars for kids. With our contribution, we aim to help make the children’s hospital experience a little brighter and more cheerful.

Learn more about the wonderful work of Fruchtalarm here:

Fruchtalarm

We wish you and your loved ones a Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year.
