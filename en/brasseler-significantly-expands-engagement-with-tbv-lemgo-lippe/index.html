Brasseler Significantly Expands Engagement with TBV Lemgo Lippe

Published on 15 July 2024

Lemgo, 15 July, 2024

We are proud to further expand our long-standing Partnership as a co-sponsor with TBV Lemgo Lippe and enter the next season together! For over 20 years, our family-owned company Brasseler has supported the Lippe-based handball Bundesliga team.

Our Komet logo will continue to prominently adorn the jerseys of TBV players in the upcoming season. Additionally, we will be present with LED boards and floor advertising in the center circle of the playing field.

“The close partnership with TBV Lemgo Lippe is a central component of our engagement for the region. With the Komet logo, which is now also visible on the players’ jerseys, we clearly show our commitment to handball and our connection to Lemgo. We are excited about the upcoming season and look forward to many successful moments together on and off the field,” emphasizes Klaus Rübesamen, managing director.

We are ready for an exciting season full of passion and success!
