Brasseler donates dental instruments to South Africa

Published on 07 May 2024

Lemgo, 07 May, 2024

As a MedTech company, we want to contribute to improving healthcare worldwide. We firmly believe that our commitment has a long-term positive impact on global society.

On the initiative of our owner Anke Niehus, we recently donated Komet instruments for dental treatments to a South African elementary school and daycare center in Paternoster (Western Cape).

“Thank you so much, Gebr. Brasseler and Komet Dental, for donating more than 1,000 dental instruments for improving the dental health of our pupils at the Paternoster school and kids at childcare here in South Africa”, says Karen Paulse / Department of Health and Wellness South Africa. ”We are grateful that you stepped up to support us. Thanks to your donation, we are able to treat 180 children which is a big step towards health education and well-being.”

In our picture story, we take you on the exciting journey of our instruments from the Lemgo logistics center to the South African school.

Many thanks to our dedicated team and especially to our partner dental practice KU64 Dr. Ziegler & Partner Zahnärzte in Berlin, who executed the donation initiative with WESTCOAST KIDS and BIG SMILE e.V.

Best wishes to Karen Paulse and her colleagues on site – you are doing a great job.
